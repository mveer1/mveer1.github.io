GEN 0
POP 0

// conway's game of life — four rules

Every cell is either alive or dead. Each generation, every cell looks at its 8 neighbours and applies these rules at the same time:

  • Underpopulation — a live cell with fewer than 2 live neighbours dies.
  • Survival — a live cell with 2 or 3 live neighbours lives on.
  • Overpopulation — a live cell with more than 3 live neighbours dies.
  • Reproduction — a dead cell with exactly 3 live neighbours becomes alive.

That's the whole game. Nothing is random and nothing is aimed — every pattern you see is those four rules running over and over.

// how to use this

  • Click or drag on the grid to paint cells alive or dead.
  • PLAY runs generations continuously; STEP advances exactly one.
  • RANDOM scatters a fresh seed; CLEAR empties the grid.
  • PRESET loads a known pattern — try Glider (it walks diagonally) or Gosper Glider Gun (it emits gliders forever).

The grid wraps at the edges, so patterns leaving one side reappear on the other.
